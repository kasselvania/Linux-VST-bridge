The two completed source-owned 128 runs had maximum sampled temperatures of 48.5°C and 49.6°C with no current thermal/power warning. The later live status read showed 53.8°C and no current power or thermal warning. The observed aggregate gap counts do not reveal the longest individual uninterrupted loss. Digitalis still reported 4,096 latency frames at 128 quantum; the bridge reported 128 more. These are 85.33 ms and 2.67 ms at 48 kHz, respectively, and are not a physical round-trip measurement.

The operator's first Bitwig HW FX round-trip readout through MOTU 8A, Pi and ShieldXL was 275.79 ms. After further operator-led testing, the operator attributed a substantial part of that result to Bitwig and reported Digitalis at approximately 90 ms. The repository has no raw analog capture or detailed isolation procedure for that final figure, so it is retained as an operator-reported physical result, not an independently reproduced measurement or proof of sub-10-ms pedal latency. The current Digitalis development check is complete for now; 128-frame delivery remains non-clean and the precise analog-path accounting is not established here.
